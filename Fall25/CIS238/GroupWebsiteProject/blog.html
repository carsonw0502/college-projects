The Best Coffee Blends for Every Brewing Method

By: Sydney Rodgers

When it comes to brewing the perfect cup of coffee, the beans you choose matter just as much as the method.

Espresso is bold, concentrated, and demands a blend that can stand up to high pressure and short extraction times. We recommend our Espresso Tempo or Valeroso beans for the best espresso.

Drip coffee is one of the most popular brewing methods, and it highlights clarity and smoothness. We recommend our Europa Blend or Black Bear Diner Blend for the best drip coffee.

Pour-over brewing emphasizes precision and flavor clarity. We recommend Colombia Del Alma or our Espresso Tempo for the best pour over coffee.

What Makes Small-Batch Coffee Roasting Different?

By: Bethany Joe

Large roasters process thousands of pounds of coffee at once. It’s fast, efficient, and built for volume, but the trade-off is usually taste. With that much coffee in one batch, it’s impossible to adjust for the subtle differences in each bean or harvest.

By roasting in small batches at our Sacramento coffee roaster, we can fine-tune every roast. Heat, airflow, and timing are all carefully adjusted to bring out the best in each origin. That’s how a bright, citrusy Colombian roast stays vibrant, while a chocolatey Guatemalan roast comes out rich and smooth—true to its character instead of flattened into a generic flavor.

Coffee Harvest Season

By: Libby Shaw

The coffee harvesting season varies depending on where the coffee is grown.

  • In Central America, harvest usually runs from November through March.
  • In South America, you’ll often see harvest between April and September.
  • In Africa and Asia, the timing shifts again depending on climate and altitude.
